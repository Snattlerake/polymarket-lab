System notes

Read before interpreting results
Historical candles are not historical order books
A price-history observation cannot prove that a simulated order could have filled at that price. Current order-book snapshots are collected prospectively and build a more useful execution dataset over time.
Signals are not profits
This initial version records research observations and parameter scores. It does not claim realized P&L, does not place orders, and does not establish that any strategy is profitable.
Related titles are not proven arbitrage
Nested-market candidates require manual verification of exact resolution rules and logical implication before they can be treated as guaranteed-payoff relationships.
